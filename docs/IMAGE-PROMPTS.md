# Image prompts and destinations

The current UI uses authored SVGs so the demo does not depend on unstable stock-image URLs. If you want a more photographic portfolio version, use these prompts and keep the images synthetic/illustrative until replaced with licensed material.

## 1. Hero field plate

**Prompt**

> Editorial documentary photograph of a solar field operations coordinator reviewing a rugged clipboard beside rows of photovoltaic panels at golden hour, subtle amber sunlight on dark green workwear, no visible logos, no readable personal data, no brand names, wide 16:9 composition with generous dark negative space on the left for interface copy, realistic but restrained, premium operations case-study photography, crisp detail, natural shadows, muted forest green and sunlit gold palette.

**Save as** `portal/assets/solar-field-hero.webp` (recommended 1600×900, quality 82).

## 2. Tool custody still life

**Prompt**

> Top-down editorial still life of a solar maintenance tool kit: torque wrench, fall-arrest harness, panel cleaning brush, labeled blank inventory tags, and a small rugged tablet on a dark workbench, directional warm sunlight, forest green and oxidized cyan accents, no readable text, no logos, premium industrial documentation photography, 4:3 composition.

**Save as** `portal/assets/tool-custody.webp` (recommended 1200×900, quality 82).

## 3. Portfolio cover texture

**Prompt**

> Abstract macro photograph of photovoltaic cell geometry catching a narrow band of warm sunlight, deep midnight green shadows, fine conductive lines, no text, no logos, sophisticated editorial technology texture, high contrast, seamless-feeling crop, 3:2 composition.

**Save as** `portal/assets/panel-texture.webp` (recommended 1200×800, quality 82).

Do not use generated people, customer documents, license plates, or company marks as real operational evidence. Treat these as visual atmosphere only.
